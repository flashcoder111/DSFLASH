## Media notes

The 13 November announcement was supplied as text and price data without attached screenshots, video or animated GIFs. All four visuals above are original SVG illustrations created for this article. Every label is in English, so no image contains Chinese text. No third-party image is reproduced here, and no watermark removal was required or attempted.

The worked cost model uses these stated assumptions: 800-token prompt, 300-token answer, 500 tokens per search result, Flash input at $0.14 per million tokens, Flash output at $0.28 per million tokens, and self-hosted index hosting at $1.00 per 1,000 queries. The last figure is a planning assumption, not a vendor price, and is labeled as such in the article and in the chart.
